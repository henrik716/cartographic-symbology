[[req-hill-shading-hill-shading]]
===== Symbolizer `hillShading` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hill-shading/hill-shading
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `hillShading` Symbolizer property as an object including `factor`, `sun`, `colorMap` and `opacityMap` properties.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support portraying an elevation coverage with a shade value computed for each cell as the dot product of the unit normal of the terrain surface and the unit vector pointing towards the sun, clamped to the range 0 to 1, where 0 is full shade (the sun at 90 degrees or more from the normal) and 1 is no shade (the sun in the direction of the normal).
====

[[req-hill-shading-factor]]
===== HillShading `factor` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hill-shading/factor
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `factor` HillShading property as a float.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support multiplying the horizontal components of the terrain surface normal by the `factor` property before normalizing it, so that a `factor` of 1 shades the terrain as it is and a larger `factor` exaggerates the relief.
====

[[req-hill-shading-sun]]
===== HillShading `sun` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hill-shading/sun
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `sun` HillShading property as an object consisting of `azimuth` and `elevation` angle members representing the orientation of the sun relative to the terrain.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support computing the shade of each cell for a light source at the `azimuth` angle (relative to North) and `elevation` angle (relative to the ground) specified by the `sun` property.
====

[[req-hill-shading-color-map]]
===== HillShading `colorMap` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hill-shading/color-map
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `colorMap` HillShading property as an array of `value` (float) and `color` (Color) pairs.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support portraying each cell with the color mapped from its computed shade value by the `colorMap`, as for the `colorMap` Symbolizer property.
====

[[req-hill-shading-opacity-map]]
===== HillShading `opacityMap` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hill-shading/opacity-map
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `opacityMap` HillShading property as an array of `value` (float) and `opacity` (float) pairs.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support portraying each cell with the opacity mapped from its computed shade value by the `opacityMap`, as for the `opacityMap` Symbolizer property.
====
