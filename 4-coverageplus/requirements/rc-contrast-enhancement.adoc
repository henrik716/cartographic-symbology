===== Symbolizer `contrastEnhancement` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/contrast-enhancement/contrast-enhancement
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `contrastEnhancement` Symbolizer property with a value of `none`, `normalize` or `histogram`.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support portraying a coverage without modifying its contrast when `contrastEnhancement` is `none`.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support stretching the contrast of a coverage linearly, so that the dimmest value is portrayed as black and the brightest value as white, when `contrastEnhancement` is `normalize`.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support stretching the contrast of a coverage based on a histogram of its brightness levels, so that each output brightness level is used by an approximately equal number of cells, when `contrastEnhancement` is `histogram`.
====
