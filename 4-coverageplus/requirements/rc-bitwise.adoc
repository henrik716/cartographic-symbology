[[req-bitwise-bit-and]]
===== Bitwise `bitAnd` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/bitwise/bit-and
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `bitAnd` operation expression taking two integer operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `bitAnd` operation expression as the bitwise AND of its two operands.
====

[[req-bitwise-bit-or]]
===== Bitwise `bitOr` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/bitwise/bit-or
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `bitOr` operation expression taking two integer operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `bitOr` operation expression as the bitwise inclusive OR of its two operands.
====

[[req-bitwise-bit-xor]]
===== Bitwise `bitXor` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/bitwise/bit-xor
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `bitXor` operation expression taking two integer operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `bitXor` operation expression as the bitwise exclusive OR of its two operands.
====

[[req-bitwise-bit-not]]
===== Bitwise `bitNot` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/bitwise/bit-not
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `bitNot` operation expression taking a single integer operand.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `bitNot` operation expression as the bitwise complement of its operand.
====

[[req-bitwise-left-shift]]
===== Bitwise `leftShift` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/bitwise/left-shift
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `leftShift` operation expression taking two integer operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `leftShift` operation expression as the first operand with its bits shifted to the left by the number of positions given by the second operand.
====

[[req-bitwise-right-shift]]
===== Bitwise `rightShift` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/bitwise/right-shift
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `rightShift` operation expression taking two integer operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `rightShift` operation expression as the first operand with its bits shifted to the right by the number of positions given by the second operand.
====
