[[req-hsv-modifier-hsv-modifier]]
===== Symbolizer `hsvModifier` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hsv-modifier/hsv-modifier
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining an `hsvModifier` Symbolizer property as an HSVModifier object including `hue`, `saturation` and `value` properties.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support applying the `hsvModifier` to the colors resulting from portraying the coverage, by converting them to the hue, saturation and value color model, modifying them, and converting them back.
====

[[req-hsv-modifier-hue]]
===== HSVModifier `hue` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hsv-modifier/hue
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `hue` HSVModifier property as a double between -360 and 360 (degrees).
part:: A Renderer SHALL support adding the `hue` value to the hue of the colors, wrapping the result around to remain within 0 to 360 degrees.
====

[[req-hsv-modifier-saturation]]
===== HSVModifier `saturation` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hsv-modifier/saturation
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `saturation` HSVModifier property as a double.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support multiplying the saturation of the colors by the `saturation` value, clamping the result to the valid range of saturation.
====

[[req-hsv-modifier-value]]
===== HSVModifier `value` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/hsv-modifier/value
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `value` HSVModifier property as a double.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support multiplying the value (brightness) of the colors by the `value` property, clamping the result to the valid range of value.
====
